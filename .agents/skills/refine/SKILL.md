# Feature Refinement

Use this skill only as a Codex entry point to the canonical roles. Load and follow
`AGENTS.md`, `agent-system/README.md`,
`agent-system/roles/spec-writer.md` for product requirements or
`agent-system/roles/architect.md` for technical design, plus
`agent-system/workflows/lifecycle.yaml`, the shared policies,
`agent-system/contracts/feature-spec-template.md`,
`agent-system/contracts/handoff.md`, `features/INDEX.md`, and the assigned feature
specification.

Select one canonical role for the requested refinement and stay within its scope. Do
not silently mix roles, broaden the feature, or change lifecycle status without the
required approval.
